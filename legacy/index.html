FAQ

Good to know before you book

Can't find what you are looking for? Send us a message and we will happily help.

Ask a question
How far in advance should I book?

Weddings book up early, so get in touch as soon as you have a date. Portrait and family sessions can often be arranged within a few weeks.

Do you shoot on location?

Yes. We photograph in the studio and on location. Travel beyond the local area is quoted individually.

When will I receive my photos?

Most sessions are delivered within two to three weeks. Weddings can take a little longer, and we will always give you a date upfront.

What should I wear?

Choose outfits you feel comfortable in. Soft, solid colours photograph beautifully, and we are happy to help you plan before the day.

Can I print my photos?

Yes. You receive high-resolution images that are ready for printing and sharing.

    Family sessions: details & terms

    What is included

    Family sessions are priced at £200 per hour of session time. Each session includes relaxed, unposed direction, a private online gallery and print-ready, professionally edited images. Sessions can take place at home, outdoors or in the studio.

    Booking and payment

    Your session is confirmed once your booking has been made and payment received. The total depends on the number of hours you book.

    Session length and timing

    The clock starts at the agreed start time, so late arrivals shorten the session. Extra time can be added on the day, subject to availability, at the same hourly rate.

    Rescheduling and cancellation

    You can reschedule free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice may not be refundable.

    Weather

    If the weather is poor for an outdoor session, we will happily move it to another date at no extra cost.

    Delivery of your images

    Your edited gallery is delivered within two to three weeks of the session.

    Copyright and usage

    Copyright stays with Lens Theory. You receive a personal licence to print and share your images. We may feature images in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

    Children

    A parent or guardian must be present for the whole session.
